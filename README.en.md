# DailyBrief

DailyBrief is a daily-updated web brief that gathers technology news, AI updates, new papers, market signals, financial news, world events, and community discussions into one readable static page.

Live site: [https://xb-bogger.github.io/DailyBrief/](https://xb-bogger.github.io/DailyBrief/)

## What It Covers

- **Tech**: GitHub Trending, AI media, and notable AI-related discussions from X.
- **Daily Papers**: Currently focused on arXiv `Cryptography and Security`, including paper title, first author and affiliation when available, keywords, and a Chinese introduction.
- **Markets**: Stocks, crypto assets, Hong Kong equities, commodities, FX, and macro indicators with technical signals and AI commentary.
- **Finance**: Sources such as Bloomberg, WSJ, FT, BBC Business, and The Economist.
- **World**: International news from BBC, Guardian, NYT, NPR, Al Jazeera, The Diplomat, and related sources.
- **Community**: V2EX / LinuxDo in Chinese mode, and Hacker News / Reddit sources in English mode.

## Why It Exists

DailyBrief is meant to reduce the daily scanning cost. It does not replace original articles or papers; instead, it gives you a clean first pass with summaries, keywords, signals, and direct source links so you can decide what deserves deeper reading.

The site is statically deployed, requires no login, uses no database, and does not depend on third-party reading services.
